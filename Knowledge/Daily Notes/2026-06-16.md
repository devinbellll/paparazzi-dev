## Morning — Added Butterworth filters to guidance_mfc


Did not get to the bottom of the weird discontinuities in the X and Y position estimation, but assuming its a real possibility from the EKF is the safest assumption. Fixed this issue by the following:

Added `Butterworth2LowPass` filtering to `guidance_mfc.c` (mirroring `guidance_indi.c` pattern):
- `filt_pos_ned[0/1]`: low-pass x/y NED position before feeding gx/gy MFC controllers (default 3 Hz, `GUIDANCE_MFC_FILTER_CUTOFF`)
- `filt_thrust`: low-pass the gz thrust output, but only for the `accel_to_att_sp()` tilt-scaling denominator — stabilizer still gets raw `thrust_cmd`
- gz (vertical) deliberately left with **raw NED z** — no position filter on altitude
- Filters re-seeded at mode entry to avoid step transients
- **Note:** `mfc_thrust_physical = -12` (hack from commit `9af03cff2`) still in place — filter wired but bypassed pending attitude saturation investigation

See [[2026-06-16-guidance-mfc-filters]]



The MFC guidance is now running for X Y and Z simultaneously which is awesome. It is much more complicated to tune than in simulation.
