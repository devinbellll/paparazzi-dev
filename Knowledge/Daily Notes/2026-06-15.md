## Afternoon — Yaw oscillation debug (MFC guidance + INDI stab)

- ANTON_MFC oscillated in yaw in NPS; ANTON (indi guidance) did not — same stab module/gains.
- **Cause 1:** `guidance_mfc` hardcoded yaw setpoint to `0.0` and never seeded `nav.heading` on entry.
- **Cause 2 (dominant):** `PERIODIC_FREQUENCY` was 500 Hz vs 1000 Hz on ANTON — INDI gains are frequency-dependent, hit the rate-sensitive G2-driven yaw axis hardest.
- Ruled out: thrust→WLS magnitude/sign, WLS priorities, gz filter, absolute vs incremental thrust setpoint.
- Cracked it by diffing the two airframe XMLs end-to-end once code-path theories were exhausted.
- Full writeup: [[2026-06-15-yaw-oscillation-mfc-indi]].
