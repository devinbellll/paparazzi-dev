## Morning — Yaw

Here I am comparing the torque and thrust for 1 motor. Due to the counter torque from the derivative, the yaw torque is much faster and leads the thrust. It's magnitude also leads me to believe my estimate of alpha for yaw is wrong as I wasn't accounting for the derivative torque in that calculation.

However it seems like the problem with my yaw axis is actually the opposite than what I thought yesterday. This is a bit confusing as it seems weird that you want longer integration window for faster dynamics.

The yaw torque actually leads the command itself, so yes feedforward seems very useful here.

## Afternoon — Upgrading Generic Quad Sim

Went ahead and started fully porting the simulation to run off actual force and torque, rather than control effectiveness estimate.

The plant now actually mirrors whats in the anton.xml JSBSim config, using the combination of the washout filtered derivative with max Force/Torque values.

Gautier also confirmed that the washout filter as present in Anton jsbsim is a physical measurement of behaviour, not an implementation particularity. I.e. the washout filter is what the torque derivative looks like IRL
