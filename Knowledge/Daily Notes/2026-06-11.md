## Morning — MFC Z lands

- **Moved MFC timing per-axis:** each SISO axis now owns its clock in `struct MfcParameters` (`mfc_siso_init` / `mfc_siso_reset`); deleted the global `struct Mfc` singleton so axes reset independently.
- **Removed the global-clock workarounds (bug-033):** dropped the bug-030 enter-split and the bug-031 `!in_flight` ground-gating — per-axis reset makes them unnecessary ("full raw MFC").
- **Made the guidance→stabilizer thrust hand-off unit-correct:** reverted `stabilization_indi.c` to stock, moved the raw-physical thrust read into `stabilization_mfc.c`, and added `GUIDANCE_MFC_THRUST_TO_PPRZ` + a derived scale (`1/Σ Bwls[3] = −166.67`) so MFC's physical thrust maps exactly into stock INDI's Bwls path (un-hacks bug-035/037).
- **bug-032 resolved by tuning:** GZ retuned so the MFC output enters the WLS `indi_v[3]` objective at the right scale and sign — MFC Z now hovers smoothly in NPS.
